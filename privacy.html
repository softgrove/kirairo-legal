Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026 · Last updated: September 25, 2026

Privacy-first by design. Everything you add stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sync, share, or sell your information. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Who we are

Kirairo ("the app", "we") is provided by Softgrove, an independent developer based in Japan. Contact: softgrove@duck.com.

What data the app handles

You may add the people you support ("your oshi"), their member colors, dates such as birthdays, anniversaries and comebacks, and photos you choose ("your entries"). Your entries are stored only locally on your device using Apple's on-device database, within an app group container so the widget can display them, protected with iOS file protection. We never receive this data.

We do not collect: your name, email, account credentials (the app has no account), location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Photos

Any photos you add are chosen by you from your own library, stay on your device, and are never uploaded to us or any third party. You are responsible for the images you choose to add (see the Terms of Use).

Widgets

Home and lock screen widgets render your entries directly on your device. No widget data leaves the device.

Notifications

If you enable reminders (for countdowns, anniversaries, or milestones), the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to us or any third party.

Deletion

You can permanently delete any oshi — together with its dates and photo — at any time from within the app. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Kirairo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and collects no information from anyone.

Third parties

The app uses only Apple system frameworks. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or external servers.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions: softgrove@duck.com.
